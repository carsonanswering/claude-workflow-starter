# Signal ranking

Reached from `SKILL.md` step 5, when a run's delta is bigger than one post
(~40 lines per doctrine §5). Lead with these in order and cut from the bottom.

A tier-one alert (SKILL.md step 2) sits above this list entirely: it leads the
post and is never cut.

Market facts below are anchored to the **2026-07-24 watchlist seed** (`_seeded`
in `watchlist.json`). The per-company `note` and `funding` fields in
`watchlist.json` are the live copy — where one of them contradicts a line here,
the note wins and this file is the stale one.

1. **Platform bundling** — a big-co folding this capability into an existing
   seat. Atlassian Rovo already went free-with-paid-plan (as of the 2026-07-24
   seed). Fastest route to the category's pricing floor collapsing, and the
   least reversible. The leading edge shows up in release notes, not pricing
   pages.
2. **Acquisition, shutdown, or pivot** of a `direct` competitor. Three of the
   seeded names had already exited as of the 2026-07-24 seed (Dashworks→HubSpot,
   Sana→Workday, Q Business sunsetting). Guru is the live candidate — six years
   since its last round (Series C, Apr 2020).
3. **Positioning shift toward "company brain" / "system of record" / "knowledge
   engine"** — Otter's Apr 2026 notetaker→Conversational Knowledge Engine pivot
   is how an adjacent player becomes direct overnight. Diff homepage H1s.
4. **Funding ≥$50M, and down-marks.** Gong's $7.25B (2021 Series E) → $4.5B
   (Nov 2025 secondary) signals category repricing, not one company's trouble.
   Track both directions.
5. **Pricing-model structure changes**, not price changes — Glean
   flat→FlexCredits, Box→AI Units, Notion→credit metering, Guru abandoning
   public per-seat (all as of the 2026-07-24 seed).
6. **MCP surface launches** — Granola, Read AI, Fathom and Gong had all shipped
   MCP within two quarters as of the 2026-07-24 seed. MCP is how a notetaker
   becomes "an MCP for employees" without building a brain.
7. **Named model-provider partnerships** — Engram×Microsoft/Notion/Harvey is the
   template (early partners as of the 2026-07-24 seed); a Microsoft/Engram M365
   announcement is top-severity, so it runs as a tier-one alert.
8. New entrant from discovery.
9. Ordinary release notes — usually cut entirely.
